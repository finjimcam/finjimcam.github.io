Current project

Replacement Player Generator

A mixed-methods pipeline that answers a recurring scouting question: when a player leaves, who are the realistic replacements? Ranks candidates across leagues by statistical similarity — age, market value, per-90 output — pulled from Transfermarkt, API-Football and StatsBomb, then triangulates that shortlist against thematic analysis of what the role actually demands, and renders it all into a designed PDF report.
